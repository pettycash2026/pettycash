Support

Need help with PettyCash? Check the answers below, or email pettycash.help@gmail.com. I usually reply within a few business days. Please don't send receipts or financial details unless I ask for them.

Getting started

Do I need an account?

No. PettyCash has no accounts and no sign-up. Connecting Google is optional and only needed if you want to send reports from your own Gmail.

The scanner doesn't open, or the camera is black.

Allow camera access in the iPhone Settings app → PettyCash → Camera. The scanner needs a real iPhone camera. If you can't scan, you can still use Import from Photos, Import from Files or Enter Manually.

The app read the wrong amount or date.

Text recognition suggests values but can't be perfect, and PettyCash never fills in information it isn't sure about. Always check the review screen before saving. You can edit any expense later from the Receipts tab.

Which currency does PettyCash use?

The currency you choose in Settings: CAD, USD, EUR, GBP, AUD or NZD, plus your iPhone's own currency if it's another one PettyCash can record. The default is Canadian dollars in Canada and, elsewhere, your iPhone's currency when PettyCash supports it. Amounts are never converted between currencies. English and French receipts and Canadian date formats are supported.

Sending reports

How does sending from Gmail work? Can PettyCash read my email?

You connect your own Google account. PettyCash asks Google for permission to send email (gmail.send) and for your email address, which it uses to show the connected account and send from it. It cannot read, search or delete your mail. When you tap Send from Gmail, the email goes directly from your iPhone to Google, with no PettyCash server involved, and appears in your Gmail Sent folder.

How do I disconnect Google?

Tap Disconnect Gmail in Settings › Sending. PettyCash deletes its token and asks Google to revoke it. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Gmail says I've reached a sending limit.

Google limits how many emails and recipients each account can send per day. PettyCash can't change those limits. Wait up to 24 hours, or send this report with Apple Mail or the share sheet instead.

The app says "sent" but my employer didn't get it.

"Sent" means Google (or your mail app) accepted the message. It doesn't prove delivery. Check the address in Settings, look in the mailbox you sent from for a bounce notice, and ask the recipient to check their spam folder.

The report is too large to email.

PettyCash automatically prepares a smaller, email-sized copy when it can. If it's still too large, use Share PDF to send it another way (for example a file-sharing link), or split the expenses into two reports.

I don't use Gmail.

That's fine. Use Apple Mail (if a Mail account is set up on your iPhone), or share the PDF to Outlook or any other app.

Does PettyCash have its own server?

No. The developer runs no servers and never receives your data. Reports go from your iPhone to your own Gmail, or through Apple Mail or the app you pick in the share sheet.

Your data

Where is my data stored? Can you recover it?

On your iPhone (and in your own iPhone backups, if you use them). The developer has no copy, so I can't recover lost data. Use Settings → Data → Export Backup regularly and save the file somewhere safe (it contains your financial records, so keep it private).

How do I move to a new iPhone?

Restore your iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup, or export a PettyCash backup on the old iPhone and import it on the new one (Settings → Data). Google connections don't move to a new device, so connect Gmail again.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Erase Data → Erase Everything removes all data, settings and the Google connection. Deleting the app also removes its data. Files you exported or shared, and emails you sent, must be deleted where they are.

Face ID isn't working.

If Face ID fails, you can unlock with your iPhone passcode. You can turn the app lock off in Settings → Privacy & Security.

Does PettyCash work offline?

Yes. Everything except connecting Gmail and sending email works without an internet connection.
