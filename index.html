PettyCash

Scan your work receipts, see exactly how much your employer owes you, and send one clean reimbursement report, all from your iPhone.

Coming soon to the App Store · iPhone · iOS 17 or later

What PettyCash does

PettyCash is for anyone who pays for work purchases out of pocket and gets paid back later.

Scan

Apple's document scanner turns paper receipts, including long and multi-page ones, into sharp PDFs. You can also import from Photos or Files.

Read

On-device text recognition suggests the store, date and final total. You confirm every field, and nothing is guessed.

Track

Total Owed is always up to date. Statuses, partial payments with history, duplicate warnings, and company-paid purchases kept out of the total.

Report

One professional PDF: a summary, an itemized table with the total, and every receipt in date order (oldest to newest), with page numbers.

Send

Send the report from your own Gmail account, with Apple Mail, or through any app in the share sheet.

Private

No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Optional Face ID lock, and backups you export yourself.

Your data is stored on your iPhone

Your expenses, receipt scans, reports, payments and settings are stored on your device, protected by iOS Data Protection. Text recognition runs on your iPhone. The developer runs no servers and never receives your data. Data leaves the app only when you choose to send, share or export it. Read the full Privacy Policy.

How PettyCash uses Google data

Sending from Gmail is optional. If you use it, you connect your own Google account, and PettyCash requests only these permissions:

PermissionUsed for
openid, emailShowing which Google account is connected, sending from that address, sending you a copy if you ask, and recording the sender in the send history on your iPhone.
gmail.send (send email on your behalf)Sending the reimbursement email you reviewed, with your report PDF attached, when you tap Send from Gmail, and a test email to yourself when you ask for one.
  • PettyCash cannot read, search or delete your email.
  • Emails go directly from your iPhone to Google's Gmail API. There is no PettyCash server in between.
  • Your Google sign-in is stored only in your iPhone's Keychain. Disconnect any time in the app (Settings › Sending › Disconnect Gmail), or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Google user data is never sold, never used for advertising, never used to train AI models, and never read by people.

PettyCash's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Details are in our Privacy Policy.
